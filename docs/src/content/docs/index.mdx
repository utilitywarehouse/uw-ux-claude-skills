## Never lose what you've learned

You can't remember everything you've learned. Neither can your team.

Project decisions, research findings, design reasoning, where a Figma file lives, what our bundle discounts are. It all ends up scattered across docs, Slack threads, email, and other people's heads. Finding it again costs more time than it should.

With the AI Toolkit, forgetting isn't a problem. Claude saves what you tell it straight to your knowledge base, and brings it back into the conversation whenever it's relevant, not just when you ask. The more you use it, the more it learns about you and your work, so the next answer is already there instead of buried in a search.

### Everyone's on the same page

Your knowledge base also holds things shared with the whole team: product wikis, the research repository, and the design system.

A wiki gets created for you automatically the moment you start a new project. It fills up with knowledge as you and your team work.

Teach Claude something about a project, a product, a study, or the brand and design system, and every other designer gets it too. It all stays in sync, automatically, for the whole team.
