Follow these in order — they get you from nothing installed to your knowledge base up and running.

Nothing here assumes you've coded before, or know what a terminal, git, or even a wiki is.

<Steps>

1. **Read the Concepts pages — or skip ahead if you're already comfortable.** Five short pages covering the vocabulary everything else assumes: [Claude Chat, Cowork, and Code](/concepts/claude-products/) (which product this all actually runs on), [Terminal basics](/concepts/terminal-basics/), [Git and GitHub](/concepts/git-and-github/), [What is a knowledge base?](/concepts/what-is-a-knowledge-base/), and [Plugins and Skills](/concepts/plugins-and-skills/). None of them assume you've read the others.

2. **Install git, VS Code, and the Claude Code extension.** See [Before you start](/install/before-you-start/) for the full checklist, including GitHub access and Google Drive sync — Drive sync is required for everyone, not optional, since it's the only backup for your knowledge base if your Mac is lost, stolen, or dies.

3. **Install the `ux` plugin.** Two terminal commands, covered on the [Install](/install/) page.

4. **Run `/ux:setup-my-knowledge-base`.** Builds your personal knowledge base from nothing — folders, a starter `CLAUDE.md`, your own `About Me` note — and links you into the team's shared content. See the [full walkthrough](/skills/setup-my-knowledge-base/).

5. **Understand your knowledge base's structure.** [Your knowledge base](/knowledge-base/) and its sub-pages cover the folder layout, how `CLAUDE.md` and `MEMORY.md` differ, and how the shared Research Repository works.

</Steps>
