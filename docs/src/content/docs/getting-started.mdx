Follow these steps in order. By the end, your knowledge base is installed and ready to use.

Nothing here assumes you've coded before, or know what a terminal, git, or even a wiki is.

<Steps>

1. **Read the Concepts pages.** Five short pages covering the vocabulary everything else assumes you know.

2. **Install the required tools.** See [the full checklist](/install/before-you-start/) of what to install and set up.

3. **Install the `ux` plugin.** Two quick steps, covered on the [Install](/install/) page.

4. **Run the setup-my-knowledge-base skill.** Builds your personal knowledge base and links you into the team's shared content.

5. **Understand your knowledge base's structure.** Learn how it's organised, and how it connects to the team's shared content.

</Steps>
